TRAFFIC LIGHT CONTROLLER USING FSM
TIMING ANALYSIS REPORT
======================

1. CLOCK CONFIGURATION
----------------------
Clock period        : 10 ns
Clock frequency     : 100 MHz

The waveform was analyzed using the GTKWave-generated simulation
waveform file:

sim/traffic_light_controller.vcd


2. FSM TIMING SEQUENCE
----------------------

The traffic light controller follows the sequence:

NS GREEN
   ↓
NS YELLOW
   ↓
ALL RED 1
   ↓
EW GREEN
   ↓
EW YELLOW
   ↓
ALL RED 2
   ↓
NS GREEN


3. WAVEFORM-MEASURED TIMING
----------------------------

State          Start Time    End Time     Duration
---------------------------------------------------
NS GREEN          0 ns        100 ns        100 ns
NS YELLOW       100 ns        130 ns         30 ns
ALL RED 1       130 ns        150 ns         20 ns
EW GREEN        150 ns        250 ns        100 ns
EW YELLOW       250 ns        280 ns         30 ns
ALL RED 2       280 ns        300 ns         20 ns

The same timing pattern is repeated in subsequent FSM cycles.


4. CLOCK-CYCLE ANALYSIS
-----------------------

Since the clock period is 10 ns:

NS GREEN:
10 clock cycles × 10 ns = 100 ns

NS YELLOW:
3 clock cycles × 10 ns = 30 ns

ALL RED:
2 clock cycles × 10 ns = 20 ns

EW GREEN:
10 clock cycles × 10 ns = 100 ns

EW YELLOW:
3 clock cycles × 10 ns = 30 ns

ALL RED:
2 clock cycles × 10 ns = 20 ns


5. COMPLETE FSM CYCLE
---------------------

Total cycle duration:

100 ns + 30 ns + 20 ns + 100 ns + 30 ns + 20 ns

= 300 ns

Therefore, one complete traffic-light sequence takes
approximately 300 ns.


6. WAVEFORM OBSERVATIONS
------------------------

The GTKWave waveform confirms the following:

1. NS GREEN is active while EW RED is active.
2. NS GREEN changes to NS YELLOW after 100 ns.
3. An ALL-RED interval of approximately 20 ns occurs before
   the EW direction receives a green signal.
4. EW GREEN remains active for approximately 100 ns.
5. EW GREEN changes to EW YELLOW for approximately 30 ns.
6. A second ALL-RED interval of approximately 20 ns occurs.
7. The controller then returns to NS GREEN.
8. The complete sequence repeats periodically.
9. NS GREEN and EW GREEN are never active simultaneously.
10. The clock waveform has a 10 ns period throughout the simulation.


7. SAFETY VERIFICATION
----------------------

The waveform demonstrates mutually exclusive green signals:

NS_GREEN = 1 → EW_GREEN = 0
EW_GREEN = 1 → NS_GREEN = 0

The ALL-RED states provide an intermediate safety interval
between changes in traffic direction.

No simultaneous NS_GREEN and EW_GREEN condition is observed
in the analyzed waveform.


8. RESET OBSERVATION
--------------------

The waveform includes the reset signal and demonstrates the
controller's reset operation during simulation.

When reset is asserted, the FSM is returned to its initial
NS GREEN state according to the RTL reset implementation.


9. CONCLUSION
-------------

GTKWave waveform analysis confirms that the Traffic Light
Controller FSM follows the intended six-state timing sequence.

Measured state durations agree with the configured timer values:

NS GREEN    = 100 ns
NS YELLOW   =  30 ns
ALL RED 1   =  20 ns
EW GREEN    = 100 ns
EW YELLOW   =  30 ns
ALL RED 2   =  20 ns

The complete FSM cycle is approximately 300 ns.

The waveform also confirms mutually exclusive green signals
and the presence of safety intervals between traffic directions.

Therefore, the simulated timing behavior is consistent with
the designed FSM and timer architecture.
